// 01 — ABOUT

A frontend developer who thinks like a data analyst and sees like a designer.

I work at the intersection of three disciplines that are usually kept apart: building interfaces, analyzing data, and designing visuals that make both legible to real people.

That combination means I don't just implement a design — I understand why it works, and I don't just visualize data — I build the product around it. I'm a Computer Science student at Modibbo Adama University, based in Jimeta, Yola, Nigeria, currently working at Zonus Global Concept Limited as a Junior Developer, Data Analyst, and Graphics Designer.

Right now I'm finishing up Lifeguard360, a geolocation-based emergency response system — one of several projects where frontend engineering and spatial data come together.
